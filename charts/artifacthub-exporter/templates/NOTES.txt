artifacthub_collection_last_success_timestamp_seconds, not the pod status.

Check the exporter:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l {{ include "artifacthub-exporter.selectorLabels" . | replace ": " "=" | replace "\n" "," }}
