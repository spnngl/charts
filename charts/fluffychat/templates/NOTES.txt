encryption features, except on localhost.

Or, from your machine:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ include "fluffychat.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  then open http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/

The login page offers the homeserver {{ .Values.config.defaultHomeserver }} (config.defaultHomeserver). The homeserver
must allow CORS from the host serving the client: the browser talks to it directly.
